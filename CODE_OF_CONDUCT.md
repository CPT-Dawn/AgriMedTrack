# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Assume good intent. Critique the code, not the person.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, whatever their experience, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and the people who use it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't follow this code. They may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/CPT-Dawn). All reports are handled confidentially.
